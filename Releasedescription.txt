### The very first & initial release of the Study Smart application.


- **Intelligent Study Planner:** 

> Study Smart is a cross-platform mobile application that uses customizable Spaced Repetition algorithms to help students schedule reviews and optimize long-term memory retention.

- **Interactive Dashboard:** 

> The app features a dynamic hero banner, subject filter pills, a horizontal slider for today's review tasks, and a live countdown timer tracking upcoming exams down to the second.

- **Structured Timetable & Todos:** 

> Users can easily organize study routines with a weekly schedule grid and manage daily tasks using an interactive, reorderable drag-and-drop todo list.

- **Visual Progress Tracking:** 

> A monthly history calendar provides instant visual feedback—marking completed sessions with stars and missed ones with flags—allowing students to review or adjust past tasks.

- **Privacy & Offline Support:** 

> Designed with an offline-first architecture, Study Smart keeps all data stored safely on the user's device with built-in options to customize review intervals and export data.
