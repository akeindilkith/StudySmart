The Science Behind Study Smart

Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve

Have you ever spent hours studying for an exam, only to realize you've forgotten most of it a few days later? This is caused by a natural psychological phenomenon known as the Hermann Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve. Research shows that our brains rapidly discard new information, losing up to 80% of it within just 24 hours unless it is actively reviewed.

Study Smart is developed to completely solve this problem. Inspired by insights on practical, long-term memory retention, the app utilizes an international scientific methodology called Spaced Repetition. Instead of cramming blindly, Study Smart automatically calculates the exact psychological thresholds where your brain is about to forget a fact, and pushes a timely notification to remind you to review it. As shown in the graph above, every time you receive a reminder and review, your memory curve spikes straight back up to 100%, flattening the curve over time to unlock flawless permanent retention.
